Recall notice RCL-2025-001: Coughcure Syrup 100ml [RECALLED]

Recall notice RCL-2025-001 (Class I)

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation directs the immediate recall of Coughcure Syrup 100ml [RECALLED], batch BATCH-R001, pack code MED-RECALL-001, manufactured by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd (MFG-002).

Reason: Diethylene glycol contamination above permissible limit.

Pharmacies and distributors must stop sale, quarantine all stock of this batch and return it to the manufacturer. Patients who have this product should stop using it and consult a doctor.
