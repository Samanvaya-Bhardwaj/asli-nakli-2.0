Recall notice RCL-2025-002: Amoxiclav 250mg Suspension [RECALLED]

Recall notice RCL-2025-002 (Class II)

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation directs the immediate recall of Amoxiclav 250mg Suspension [RECALLED], batch BATCH-R002, pack code MED-RECALL-002, manufactured by Cipla Ltd (MFG-001).

Reason: Microbial contamination detected in reconstituted oral suspension.

Pharmacies and distributors must stop sale, quarantine all stock of this batch and return it to the manufacturer. Patients who have this product should stop using it and consult a doctor.
