Recall notice RCL-2025-003: Metrogyl 400mg [RECALLED]

Recall notice RCL-2025-003 (Class II)

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation directs the immediate recall of Metrogyl 400mg [RECALLED], batch BATCH-R003, pack code MED-RECALL-003, manufactured by Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd (MFG-003).

Reason: Sub-potent active ingredient dissolution failure.

Pharmacies and distributors must stop sale, quarantine all stock of this batch and return it to the manufacturer. Patients who have this product should stop using it and consult a doctor.
