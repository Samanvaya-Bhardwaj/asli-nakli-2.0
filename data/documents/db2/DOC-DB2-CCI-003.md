# Cold-chain incident report

Consignment DBATCH-021 of Corex Cough Syrup (batch BATCH-U002, pack code MED-UNAUTH-002) left the 15.0-25.0 C band for 4 logged interval(s); peak 33.4 C.

## Impact

The product is labelled for storage below 25-30 C; the excursion is not expected to affect quality, but the consignment is held until the manufacturer confirms.

## Corrective action

Vehicle Unmarked Auto withdrawn from cold-chain duty; refrigeration unit to be serviced; driver retraining scheduled.
