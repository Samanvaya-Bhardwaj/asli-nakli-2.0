# Cipla Ltd - Product Quality Review, H1 2025

## Scope

This review covers 4 batch(es) released from Cipla Ltd - Verna Industrial Estate Unit I, Cipla Ltd - Baddi Unit II between 01 January 2025 and 30 June 2025, under manufacturing licence MH/MFG/2018/001.

## Batches released

- Novamox 500mg (Amoxycillin), batch BATCH-A001, pack code MED-ASALI-001: released, assay within limits
- Atorva 40mg (Atorvastatin), batch BATCH-A005, pack code MED-ASALI-005: released, assay within limits
- Ciplox 500mg (Ciprofloxacin), batch BATCH-A007, pack code MED-ASALI-007: released, assay within limits
- Corex Cough Syrup (Codeine phosphate), batch BATCH-U002, pack code MED-UNAUTH-002: released, assay within limits

## Deviations and investigations

Batch BATCH-R002 of Amoxiclav 250mg Suspension [RECALLED] (MED-RECALL-002) is the subject of a regulatory recall: Microbial contamination detected in reconstituted oral suspension. A root-cause investigation into raw-material suppliers is open and further release of this formulation is on hold.

## Conclusion

The quality unit confirms that released batches met their specifications at the time of release. Stability commitments continue as planned.
