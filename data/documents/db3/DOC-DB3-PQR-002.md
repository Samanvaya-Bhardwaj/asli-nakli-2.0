# Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd - Product Quality Review, H1 2025

## Scope

This review covers 3 batch(es) released from Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd - Halol Unit I, Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd - Rangpo Unit II between 01 January 2025 and 30 June 2025, under manufacturing licence GJ/MFG/2015/002.

## Batches released

- Pacimol 500mg (Paracetamol), batch BATCH-A002, pack code MED-ASALI-002: released, assay within limits
- Falcinil 80/480mg (Artemether+Lumefantrine), batch BATCH-A004, pack code MED-ASALI-004: released, assay within limits
- Valium 5mg (Diazepam), batch BATCH-U001, pack code MED-UNAUTH-001: released, assay within limits

## Deviations and investigations

Batch BATCH-R001 of Coughcure Syrup 100ml [RECALLED] (MED-RECALL-001) is the subject of a regulatory recall: Diethylene glycol contamination above permissible limit. A root-cause investigation into raw-material suppliers is open and further release of this formulation is on hold.

## Conclusion

The quality unit confirms that released batches met their specifications at the time of release. Stability commitments continue as planned.
