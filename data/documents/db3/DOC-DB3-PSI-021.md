# Product safety information: Hepatitis B vaccine

Therapeutic class: vaccine; ATC J07BC01; schedule H.

Marketed as: Hepashield 20mcg/ml.

## Storage

Store refrigerated between 2 and 8 C; do not freeze. Discard if exposed to heat.

## Safety

Report suspected adverse reactions to the pharmacovigilance programme. Check the pack's printed code and hologram; buy only from licensed pharmacies.
