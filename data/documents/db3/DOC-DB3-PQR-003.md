# Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd - Product Quality Review, H1 2025

## Scope

This review covers 4 batch(es) released from Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd - Hyderabad Unit I, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd - Pithampur Unit II between 01 January 2025 and 30 June 2025, under manufacturing licence TS/MFG/2019/003.

## Batches released

- Metsmall 850mg (Metformin), batch BATCH-A003, pack code MED-ASALI-003: released, assay within limits
- Electral ORS (ORS Formula), batch BATCH-A006, pack code MED-ASALI-006: released, assay within limits
- Omez 20mg (Omeprazole), batch BATCH-A008, pack code MED-ASALI-008: released, assay within limits
- Calcirol 1000 IU (Cholecalciferol), batch BATCH-A010, pack code MED-ASALI-010: released, assay within limits

## Deviations and investigations

Batch BATCH-R003 of Metrogyl 400mg [RECALLED] (MED-RECALL-003) is the subject of a regulatory recall: Sub-potent active ingredient dissolution failure. A root-cause investigation into raw-material suppliers is open and further release of this formulation is on hold.

## Conclusion

The quality unit confirms that released batches met their specifications at the time of release. Stability commitments continue as planned.
